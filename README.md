The resources I actually recommend to product leaders who are bringing AI into their products and their teams. Each one earns its place by being clear, practical and still useful a year after you first read it.

## Product Leadership Foundations

AI changes the materials, not the job. These are the books I return to when a team has lost sight of the job.

- [Inspired, Empowered and Transformed](https://www.svpg.com/books/) by Marty Cagan. The clearest account of how strong product teams work, and why so many organisations struggle to let them.
- [Continuous Discovery Habits](https://www.producttalk.org/) by Teresa Torres. Weekly contact with customers, made practical. Even more important when AI makes building cheap.
- [Escaping the Build Trap](https://melissaperri.com/book) by Melissa Perri. Outcomes over output, explained for leaders who need to change how the whole organisation thinks.
- [The Squiggly Career](https://www.amazingif.com/) by Helen Tupper and Sarah Ellis. For the people side of a fast-changing field, and for anyone whose career has had a few turns.

## Understanding AI Without the Maths

- [Co-Intelligence](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/) by Ethan Mollick. The most useful book for leaders on working alongside AI, written by someone who tests everything himself.
- [Neural Networks](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/neural-networks) by 3Blue1Brown. Beautiful visual explanations of how these models learn. Watch the first two even if you skip the rest.
- [Elements of AI](https://www.elementsofai.com/) from the University of Helsinki. A free, gentle introduction for anyone in the organisation.
- [AI Glossary for Product Leaders](https://andersonco.uk/ai-glossary). My own plain-English guide to forty terms, each with a question to ask in your next meeting.

## Building AI Products

- [AI Engineering](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-engineering/9781098166298/) by Chip Huyen. The best single book on building with foundation models, from evaluation to deployment.
- [Designing Machine Learning Systems](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-machine-learning/9781098107956/) by Chip Huyen. Still the reference for the parts of ML that happen after the demo.
- [Building Effective Agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents) from Anthropic. A calm, practical case for starting simple before reaching for agents.
- [Prompt Engineering Overview](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/overview) from Anthropic. Clear guidance on writing prompts that behave reliably.
- [OpenAI Cookbook](https://cookbook.openai.com/). Worked examples for common patterns, useful for product managers who like to see how things really work.
- [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/). The open standard for connecting AI to tools and data. Worth understanding before your next integration decision.

## Evals and Quality

If you only read one section, make it this one. Evals are where AI products succeed or quietly fail.

- [Your AI Product Needs Evals](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals/) by Hamel Husain. The essay that convinced a lot of teams, including several of mine.
- [Eugene Yan](https://eugeneyan.com/) on applied ML and LLM systems. Thoughtful, evidence-led writing on evals, retrieval and product patterns.
- [AI Coach Evals](https://github.com/elleanderson/ai-coach-evals). My own small, readable eval kit, tested on a live coaching product.

## Designing for People

- [People + AI Guidebook](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook/) from Google PAIR. Practical patterns for trust, explanation and graceful failure.
- [HAX Toolkit](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/) from Microsoft. Eighteen guidelines for human and AI interaction, with examples and a workbook.

## Risk, Safety and Security

- [OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/). The security risks every AI feature should be checked against, prompt injection first among them.
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework). A sensible, widely used structure for thinking about AI risk across a product's life.

## Governance and Regulation

- [EU AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/). The full text, searchable, with summaries and the latest changes.
- [ISO/IEC 42001](https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html). The management system standard for AI, increasingly asked about in procurement.
- [AI and Data Protection](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/) from the UK Information Commissioner's Office. The guidance UK teams need before personal data meets a model.
- [AI Playbook for the UK Government](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-playbook-for-the-uk-government). Ten principles that work just as well outside government.
- [OECD AI Principles](https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles). The shared reference point behind much of the world's AI policy.
- [AI Use Case Register](https://github.com/elleanderson/ai-use-case-register). My own open template for keeping track of every AI use case, with an indicative EU AI Act triage.

## Newsletters Worth Opening

- [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/). The broadest, most generous product newsletter, with regular deep dives on AI.
- [One Useful Thing](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/) by Ethan Mollick. Experiments and research on what AI means for work, written for everyone.
- [Simon Willison's Weblog](https://simonwillison.net/). Daily, hands-on notes from one of the most careful voices on AI tools and their risks.
- [Mind the Product](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/). Talks, articles and a community for product people around the world.

## Free Courses

- [Machine Learning Crash Course](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course) from Google. A sound grounding in how machine learning works.
- [Practical Deep Learning](https://course.fast.ai/) from fast.ai. Hands-on and refreshingly honest about what really matters.
- [Short Courses](https://www.deeplearning.ai/short-courses/) from DeepLearning.AI. Focused hour-long courses on prompting, agents, retrieval and evals.

## Tools and Templates From Me

- [AI Feature Health Dashboard](https://github.com/elleanderson/ai-feature-health-dashboard). One page that tells leaders whether a live AI feature is affordable, fast, good and safe.
- [UK Copy Checker](https://github.com/elleanderson/uk-copy-checker). A house style checker for UK English, as a command-line tool and a GitHub Action.
- [Product Leadership Toolkit](https://github.com/elleanderson/-product-leadership-toolkit). Twenty-one free, print-ready tools for product leaders.
- [New Leaf](https://andersonco.uk/newleaf). A free, private coaching tool for telling a squiggly career story with confidence.
