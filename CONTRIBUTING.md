# Contributing

Thank you for suggesting something. This list stays useful because it stays short.

## What Gets In

- Something you've read, used or recommended yourself, more than once
- Practical for people leading product, as well as for engineers
- Still useful a year from now
- Free to read, or a book worth buying

## What Doesn't

- Your own product, unless it's free, open and genuinely the best of its kind
- Listicles, hype pieces and anything behind an email wall
- Links that need an account to read

## How to Suggest Something

Open a pull request that adds one line in the right section, in this form:

```markdown
- [Name](https://example.com) by Author. One or two sentences on why a product leader should read it.
```

Every pull request is checked for broken links and for UK house style. Please keep descriptions in UK English.
